# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Streamlit, pandas, scikit-learn. Visual language pinned to Quayside (Plus Jakarta Sans, cream/paper/ink, ultramarine) in `static/quayside.css`.

## Users

Hackathon team operating a food surplus workflow: load inventory, inspect stock, and later predict surplus, categorize food, match organizations, and plan distribution.

## Product Purpose

Move surplus food from donors to recipient organizations with prediction, categorization, matching, and impact tracking.

## Constraints

- Do not add, remove, or rewrite existing UI copy or features.
- Inventory CSV schema: item_id, item_name, category, quantity_kg, expiry_date, location, donor, status.
- Surplus prediction, categorization, matching, and distribution remain unimplemented stubs.
